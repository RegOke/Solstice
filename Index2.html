Sunday the 21st of June · The Longest Night

Ye Wynter Solstice Revel

Hark, traveller. The darke half of the yeare endeth, and the hearth hungereth.

🕯️ Doores at six · 🔥 The fyre dieth not · 🍷 Mull'd cider

How thou shalt be enrolled

  1. Inscribe thy name beneath the little wooden manling.
  2. Drag the manling into the hearth-fyre with thine own finger.
  3. When the flames leap, thou art bound to the revel. There is no other way.
The roll of soules is being fetched from the embers…
⬆ Drag me into the fyre, and thy place is kept ⬆
One manling per soule · Bring a sorrow to burn · Telle no one what the window saw
✦ admit one soule ✦

It is done.
