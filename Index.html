Upon the XXI of December · The Longest Night

Ye Wynter
Solstice Revel

Hark, weary traveller. The darke half of the yeare draweth to its ende, and the hearth hungereth.

🕯️ Doores open at six o' the clock · 🔥 The fyre dieth not · 🍷 Mull'd wine for every soule

How thou shalt be enrolled

  1. Inscribe thy name beneath the little wooden manling.
  2. Drag the manling into the hearth-fyre with thine own finger.
  3. When the flames leap and his bones glow amber, thou art bound to the revel. There is no other way.
